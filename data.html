novaa-off-wines.csv – Weingut, Wein, Weintyp, NOVAA-Stil, Land und Strichcodes · Lizenz und Änderungen

Was wir verändert haben

Nur Traubenweine übernommen (ohne Kochwein, Essig, Mischgetränke), Namen bereinigt, Weintyp und NOVAA-Stil zugeordnet, Strichcodes auf EAN-8/EAN-13 vereinheitlicht und mehrere Codes desselben Weins zusammengefasst.

Open data

For supermarket wines and barcodes NOVAA uses data from Open Food Facts (© Open Food Facts contributors), licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0). We make the derived NOVAA data available under the same licence.

novaa-off-wines.csv – producer, wine, type, NOVAA style, country and barcodes · licence and changes

What we changed

Kept grape wines only (no cooking wine, vinegar or mixed drinks), cleaned names, assigned wine type and NOVAA style, normalised barcodes to EAN-8/EAN-13 and merged several codes of the same wine.

Other sources

Wines and producers: LWIN © Liv-ex (CC BY 4.0). Grapes and regions: Wikidata (CC0) and eAmbrosia (European Commission). Fallback bottle photos: Open Food Facts (CC BY-SA 3.0).
